# Font notice clarification

This note applies to the bundled DeparturePixelZh Compact font. The producer's
NOTICE and the original component notices and provenance remain unchanged.

The producer NOTICE identifies Cubic 11 as 1.500. The retained provenance for
the actual pinned Cubic 11 input records embedded version 1.451, SHA-256
`0193f5f033612496df6b45ee92ac3b335bc6a5a24ff95da55ca87b33e57dcf62`, at
revision `4c566f7d6cc5c05ee360fe9cff56b5da1fcafd4d`. The version 1.500 also
appears in that provenance for Departure Mono. Do not infer the version of
other Cubic 11 releases from this record.

For Font Logos 1.3.0, the producer NOTICE and retained `Licenses/FontLogos.txt`
identify the Unlicense, while the retained Nerd Fonts component readme and audit
describe it as "unlicensed". Preserve all of those source texts. This
discrepancy remains under review; this note does not resolve its legal effect.
Brand marks remain subject to their respective owners' trademark rights.
